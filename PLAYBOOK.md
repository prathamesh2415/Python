# The Free Faceless Story Reel System

Build and run a faceless Instagram page that tells high-share stories using only free tools.

**Target time:** under 1 hour per reel once you have the rhythm.  
**Format:** 9:16 vertical, 20–45 seconds, big text, emotional story, no face on camera.

---

## 0. What you are actually selling to the algorithm

Not “nice editing.” Not “AI magic.”

You are posting **stories people feel compelled to share**.

Filter every idea through this (skip if it fails all three):

1. **Shareable** — someone tags a friend / forwards it  
2. **Saveable** — someone wants to rewatch or remember it  
3. **Emotional** — shock, awe, injustice, hope, fear, pride, curiosity payoff

Interesting ≠ viral. **Shareable** = growth.

---

## 1. Pick a niche (do this once)

Best niches for this workflow:

- History / mythology / forgotten true stories  
- Crime / mystery with a twist ending  
- Science / space / “did you know” with a punch  
- Money / scams / “how they got rich” narratives  
- Local culture legends (works well in India / regional languages)

**Rules:**

- One niche per page  
- Audience must already exist on Instagram (search the niche + “reel”)  
- You can research faster than you can invent from scratch  

Write your niche in one line:

> “Faceless page that tells [TYPE] stories for [WHO], in [LANGUAGE].”

Example: *“Faceless page that tells shocking forgotten Indian history stories for Hindi + English viewers.”*

## 2. Account setup (day 0)

Use `checklists/account-setup.md`.

Minimum:

- New Instagram account (Creator or Business)  
- Username that matches the niche  
- Profile photo: symbol / stamp / illustration (not your face)  
- Bio: what stories you tell + soft CTA  
- Post 0–2 pinned “about this page” style reels later; first priority is story content  

Do **not** wait for a perfect brand kit. Ship content.

## 3. Free tool stack (locked)

Use this exact stack unless a free tool dies:

| Job | Tool | Free reality (Nov 2026) |
|---|---|---|
| Find + expand stories | ChatGPT free or Gemini free | Prompt pack in `/prompts` |
| Generate still frames | Bing Image Creator **or** Gemini image | Same style prompt every time |
| Animate frames → clips | **Google Flow** (primary) | ~50 credits/day, short clips; avoid peak hours |
| Backup video gen | Dreamina free daily credits | Use if Flow is rate-limited |
| Edit reel | CapCut free or IG Edits | Auto captions; avoid Pro assets |
| Music | IG trending audio | Pick audio *after* rough cut |
| Caption | ChatGPT/Gemini | `/prompts/05-caption.md` |

Fallback if video gen is empty that day:

- Still images + Ken Burns zoom in CapCut  
- Free stock from Pexels/Pixabay (no face needed)  
- Text-first “documentary card” style (still works if story is strong)

## 4. The 10-step reel pipeline

### Step 1 — Find potential stories (5–10 min)

Sources (free):

- Wikipedia “Did you know” / obscure history pages  
- Old newspaper archives / public domain cases  
- Reddit story subs (verify facts before posting)  
- Books summaries / public lectures  
- Your own niche note bank

Prompt: `prompts/01-find-stories.md`

Output: 5 candidate one-liners. Pick **one**.

### Step 2 — Kill weak stories (2 min)

Ask:

- Would I share this with a friend?  
- Is there a twist, injustice, or “wait… what?” moment by second 8?  
- Can I show it visually without a talking head?

If no → next candidate. Do not force a weak story with better editing.

### Step 3 — Expand into a reel script (8–12 min)

Structure (20–40s spoken / text pace):

1. **Hook (0–3s):** open loop / shocking claim  
2. **Setup (3–12s):** who / when / stakes  
3. **Turn (12–25s):** the twist or reveal  
4. **Payoff (25–35s):** ending + emotional beat  
5. **CTA (last 2–3s):** follow for more stories / part 2

Prompt: `prompts/02-expand-story.md`

Write on-screen lines short: **4–8 words per card**.

### Step 4 — Break into visual scenes (5 min)

Turn script into 5–8 scene cards:

- Scene #  
- On-screen text  
- Image prompt  
- Motion note (slow zoom / pan / particle dust / rain)

Prompt: `prompts/03-scene-breakdown.md`

### Step 5 — Generate images (8–12 min)

Rules for consistent “page look”:

- Same aspect: **9:16** or generate square and crop later  
- Same style line in every prompt (cinematic, grain, muted palette, etc.)  
- No celebrity likeness, no logos you don’t own  
- Prefer illustrated / painterly / documentary stills for history niches  

Save files as `01.png`, `02.png`, …

### Step 6 — Animate (10–15 min)

In Google Flow (or Dreamina):

- Image-to-video when possible (more consistent than text-only)  
- Aim for **4–8s clips**  
- Generate only what you need (credits are daily and don’t roll over)  
- Skip peak congestion windows when free video is blocked  

If credits run out: CapCut Ken Burns on stills is fine.

### Step 7 — Edit in CapCut / Edits (10–15 min)

Timeline recipe:

1. Drop clips in story order  
2. Cut dead air; keep pace snappy  
3. Big center text (high contrast)  
4. Auto captions if you use voice  
5. Soft transitions only (hard cuts often perform better)  
6. Export 1080x1920, 30fps  

**Watermark trap:** if CapCut marks an asset “Pro,” remove it.

### Step 8 — Audio (3–5 min)

- Prefer Instagram **trending** audio for your region/niche  
- Duck music under voice if narrating  
- Or go music-only + strong text (fully faceless, no voice)

### Step 9 — Caption + cover (3–5 min)

- First line of caption = hook (not “Part 1 🔥”)  
- 3–8 hashtags max, niche-specific  
- Cover frame: biggest text + clearest face-of-story visual (still no *your* face)

Prompt: `prompts/05-caption.md`

### Step 10 — Upload + review (3 min)

Post as Reel. Then log in a simple sheet:

| Date | Story title | Hook | Views 24h | Shares | Saves | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

Double down on formats that get **shares + saves**, not vanity likes.

## 5. Cadence that free tools can support

| Goal | Realistic free-stack cadence |
|---|---|
| Learning week | 1 reel / day |
| Steady growth | 1 reel / day, 5–6 days/week |
| Batching | Write 5 scripts Sunday; produce 1/day |

Do not burn all Flow credits on experiments at noon if evenings work better for you.

---

## 6. Growth loops (still free)

1. **Story quality first** — weak story never gets fixed by tools  
2. Series — “Part 1 / Part 2” when retention is high  
3. Reply to comments with a follow-up reel idea  
4. Cross-post to YouTube Shorts / Facebook Reels with the same file  
5. Collaborate shoutouts only after you have proof posts  

Paid shoutouts / paid tools are optional later — not required to start.

---

## 7. Monetization path (after traction)

Order that usually makes sense:

1. Consistency + retention  
2. Meta/Facebook Reels bonuses **if available in your region/eligibility**  
3. Brand DM outreach with a simple media kit  
4. Your own digital product (guide, prompts, niche pack)  
5. Affiliate only if it fits the niche without killing trust  

This playbook does **not** promise payouts. Eligibility and programs change.

---

## 8. Quality bar before you hit Post

- [ ] Hook is clear in first 2 seconds  
- [ ] Story passes share/save/emotion filter  
- [ ] Text is readable on a phone in sunlight  
- [ ] No Pro watermark  
- [ ] Ending has a reason to follow or watch next  
- [ ] You would share it yourself  

If two boxes fail, rewrite before uploading.

---

## 10. Legal / safety (don’t skip)

- Prefer public-domain / well-sourced historical facts; avoid defamation  
- Don’t claim medical/financial advice  
- Label AI where platform rules require it  
- Check commercial-use terms of free AI video tools before running ads  
- No scraped private content; no copyrighted movie clips
