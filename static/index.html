How This Desk Thinks

1. Business Quality

Prefer high ROE and ROA with wide operating margins — evidence of a durable economic moat and pricing power, not just a hot ticker.

2. Fortress Balance Sheet

Low debt-to-equity and solid liquidity. Buffett buys businesses that can survive storms without begging capital markets for oxygen.

3. Owner Earnings

Free cash flow is the cash a owner can extract. Positive, recurring FCF beats accounting gymnastics every time.

4. Margin of Safety

Price is what you pay; value is what you get. Intrinsic value here blends Graham number, capped growth multiples, and FCF capitalization.

5. Market Cap / GDP

The classic Buffett Indicator gauges whether the overall equity market is cheap or dear vs the economy — a climate check, not a stock picker.

Live quotes and fundamentals via Yahoo Finance (yfinance). India names use NSE .NS symbols. Screener universe covers major liquid blue chips.
