# Prompt — Animate a still (Google Flow / Dreamina)

Use as the motion / video prompt when uploading a still:

```text
Subtle cinematic motion only. Slow push-in on the subject. Gentle atmospheric movement (dust / fog / cloth / leaves) matching the scene. Keep the original composition, face identity, and clothing consistent. No morphing, no extra limbs, no text, no watermark, no sudden cuts, no camera shake.
```

Shorter variant:

```text
Slow cinematic zoom-in, soft ambient motion, keep subject identity locked, no text, no distortion.
```

Tips for free tiers:

- Prefer **image-to-video** over text-to-video for consistency  
- Generate 4–8s clips  
- Spend credits on the hook + twist scenes first  
- If blocked at peak hours, switch to CapCut Ken Burns on the stills
