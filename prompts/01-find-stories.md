# Prompt — Find viral-ready stories

Paste into ChatGPT or Gemini (free).

```text
You are a research assistant for a faceless Instagram storytelling page.

Niche: [YOUR NICHE]
Audience: [WHO + LANGUAGE]
Region focus (optional): [e.g. India / world / ancient history]

Task: Give me 10 story candidates that are likely to be SHARED, not just liked.

For each candidate provide:
1. One-line title
2. 2-sentence summary
3. The "wait… what?" twist or emotional hook
4. Why someone would share it (tag a friend / argue / feel proud / feel shocked)
5. Visual potential: Easy / Medium / Hard for AI images
6. Risk notes: copyright, living people, unverified claims
7. Score 1–10 for shareability

Rules:
- Prefer true / well-documented stories. Mark legends clearly as legends.
- No celebrity deepfakes. No medical advice. No hate.
- Avoid stories that need expensive footage to work.
- Diversify: not 10 versions of the same trope.

End with your TOP 3 picks and why.
```
